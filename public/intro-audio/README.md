# Recorded voice-overs for Penny's intro (optional)

By default, Penny speaks with the most natural voice on the viewer's device. For a real human voice:

1. Record each line below (a voice actor, yourself, or a natural-voice service such as ElevenLabs, Microsoft Azure Speech or Google Cloud Text-to-Speech). Check the service's terms allow commercial use.
2. Save them as MP3 files in this folder: `scene-1.mp3` to `scene-9.mp3`.
3. Add `NEXT_PUBLIC_INTRO_AUDIO=on` to `.env.local` and to Vercel's environment variables, then restart or redeploy.

If a file is missing, that scene falls back to the device's voice. Captions always show.

## The script

**scene-1.mp3**

> Kia ora, I'm Penny! In about a minute, I'll show you what this app can do. Plan your whole household's retirement, even across countries, and ask questions you'll get cited answers to.

**scene-2.mp3**

> Answer a few easy questions in the guided setup. It takes about five minutes. Add your savings, KiwiSaver or super, your home, your income and pensions, and the costs of children, parents and pets. Plan just for you, or for you and your partner.

**scene-3.mp3**

> Working in one country and planning to retire in another? Add savings, homes and pensions in different currencies. We use today's exchange rates and each country's pension rules.

**scene-4.mp3**

> See how your savings could grow to retirement, and how long they could last, tested across hundreds of different ups and downs in the markets.

**scene-5.mp3**

> We show what's working, what to watch, and levers you could pull, like retiring a little later, lowering fees, or a reverse mortgage. Plus a quick check of your emergency fund.

**scene-6.mp3**

> Ask questions in plain words, and get answers with sources you can check. Switch on 'include my plan' to have your own numbers reviewed against well-known investing principles and your country's rules.

**scene-7.mp3**

> See how fees add up over the years, side by side, and find free tools from trusted sources such as Sorted.

**scene-8.mp3**

> Your numbers stay on your device unless you choose to share them, and you can clear them at any time. Remember, this is education, not financial advice.

**scene-9.mp3**

> Plan your whole household's retirement, even across countries, and ask questions you'll get cited answers to. Let's get started!
