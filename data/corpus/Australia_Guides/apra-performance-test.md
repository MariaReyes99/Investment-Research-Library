# Understanding APRA's superannuation performance test

## What APRA assesses
APRA conducts an annual performance test for specified superannuation products. The test compares a product's performance with tailored benchmarks and applies consequences to products that fail the test. The scope includes MySuper products and trustee-directed products; it is not a rating of every investment option available to every member.

## How to use the result
Treat the test as one data point. It does not replace comparing investment objectives, risk, fees, insurance, service, choice options or the member's circumstances. Products can have different asset allocations, and comparisons should match like with like. Check the current APRA results and linked product information because the test is updated annually.
