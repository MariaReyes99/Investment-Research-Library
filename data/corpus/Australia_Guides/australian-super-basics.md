# Australian superannuation basics

## What super is
Superannuation is a long-term retirement savings system. Contributions and investment returns are held in a super fund, and access is generally restricted until a condition of release is met. Rules differ by age, residency, contribution type and personal circumstances.

## Choosing and monitoring a fund
Compare investment options, asset mix, fees, insurance, service and performance over appropriate periods. Check current fund disclosure and use official comparison resources where available. A fund's past performance or a product label does not establish that it is suitable for a particular person.

## Contributions, tax and access
Contribution caps, tax treatment and preservation/access rules are detailed and can change. The Australian Taxation Office is the primary source for current eligibility, tax and withdrawal rules. Confirm the current rules before making a contribution or withdrawal decision.
