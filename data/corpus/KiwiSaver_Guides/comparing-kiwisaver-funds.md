# Comparing KiwiSaver funds and providers

## Compare like with like
Start by comparing funds in the same broad category. A growth fund and a conservative fund take different levels of investment risk, so comparing their recent returns alone can be misleading. Check the fund's growth-asset mix, stated investment timeframe, risk indicator, and whether the provider actively changes the mix.

## Compare costs and performance together
Review the fund's current fees and returns over several periods, not just its latest year. Compare returns with funds using similar strategies and with an appropriate benchmark where available. Past performance is not a reliable guide to future returns. Fees reduce the amount that remains invested and can compound into a material difference over long periods.

## Read the current disclosure
Use the provider's current product disclosure statement and fund update for the exact fund option. Confirm the document date, investment mix, fees, tax treatment, withdrawal terms and any changes to the strategy. Provider names alone do not identify a comparable investment option.

## Comparison resources
- [Sorted Smart Investor: KiwiSaver and managed funds](https://www.smartinvestor.sorted.org.nz/kiwisaver-and-managed-funds/): live fund comparison; check the selected fund type and current data date.
- [Financial Markets Authority: investing](https://www.fma.govt.nz/consumer/investing/): investor guides, including managed funds, ETFs, platforms and advice.
- [FMA Managed Funds Guide](https://www.fma.govt.nz/assets/Consumer-section/Managed-Fund-Guide.pdf): further guidance on choosing and monitoring managed funds and ETFs.

This note describes a comparison process; it does not rank providers or recommend a fund for an individual. Confirm current provider information in the linked live tools and disclosure documents.
