# KiwiSaver contribution changes from 2026

## Default contribution rate from 1 April 2026
The default employee contribution rate and the matching employer rate rose from 3% to 3.5% on 1 April 2026. Employees who were already contributing more than 3% do not have their rate reduced. Eligible employees aged 16 or 17 can receive employer contributions under the updated rules.

## Temporary rate reduction
An employee who wants to remain at 3% can apply for a temporary rate reduction. Inland Revenue describes a reduction period of 3 to 12 months; the rate returns to the default when the period ends. Employers may choose to match the reduced employee rate.

## Planned change from 1 April 2028
Inland Revenue says the default employee and matching employer contribution rate is scheduled to rise to 4% on 1 April 2028. Treat this as a scheduled future change, and recheck the current rules before acting.

## Government contribution
Inland Revenue states that from 1 July 2025 the government contribution is 25 cents for each eligible dollar contributed, up to NZD 260.72 per year. People with taxable income above NZD 180,000 do not qualify. Eligibility conditions apply, so check the current Inland Revenue page.
