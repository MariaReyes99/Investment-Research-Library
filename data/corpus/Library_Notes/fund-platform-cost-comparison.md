# Comparing investment fund and platform costs

## Compare the whole fee stack
The total cost can include fund management costs, other operating costs, transaction costs, contribution or advice fees, platform/account fees, brokerage, currency conversion, spreads and withdrawal or transfer fees. Not every provider charges every fee. Check the current fee schedule and product disclosure statement rather than relying on a headline management fee.

## Make a fair comparison
Use the same opening balance, regular contribution, period and gross-return assumption to isolate cost differences. If the products have different asset mixes or risks, show those assumptions separately rather than attributing the whole return difference to the platform. Include taxes only when the comparison models them consistently.

## Interpreting projections
A fee calculator is a scenario, not a forecast. Reinvested-return assumptions, taxes, brokerage and market variability may be excluded. Small annual fee differences can compound over time, but actual returns are uncertain and past returns do not predict future returns.

## Comparison tools and guidance
- ASIC MoneySmart, [Managed funds fee calculator](https://moneysmart.gov.au/managed-funds-and-etfs/managed-funds-fee-calculator), last updated 18 June 2026. It compares how fees affect projections and notes that taxes and brokerage are not included.
- ASIC MoneySmart, [Choosing a managed fund](https://moneysmart.gov.au/managed-funds-and-etfs/choosing-a-managed-fund), last updated 1 July 2026.
- New Zealand FMA, [Investing](https://www.fma.govt.nz/consumer/investing/), with guides on managed funds, ETFs and online investing platforms.

Provider and platform fees change. Recheck source documents and date each comparison. This is educational information, not a recommendation.
